### Tips

- [Car pooling](https://gomore.dk/rides)
  - For Copenhagen to Aarhus
  - For Aarhus to Aalborg
- To stay on the lower end of this range, you'll want to prioritize shopping at discount supermarkets like:
  - **Netto**, **Fakta**, **Lidl**, **Rema 1000**
- Look out for "Tilbud" (offers/sales) in the stores.
- **"Too Good To Go" app:** This is highly recommended in Denmark. It allows you to buy surplus food from supermarkets, bakeries, and restaurants at a heavily discounted price at the end of the day. You can get a bag of groceries or a meal for a fraction of the origin  al cost.
- Donkey bike app

### Tips with CC

- Home of Carlsberg & Church of Our Saviour both require pre-booking. You can find the pre-booking links on the attraction's site the Copenhagen Card app or on their page on the website.
- Rosenborg Castle requires you to obtain a time slot by going there and getting a same-day ticket.
- You cannot pre-book The Canal Tours online. However, if you want to be sure of getting a specific timeslot, you can stop by their box-office at Ved Stranden and pre-book there upon arrival.
- Please only pre-book ONE timeslot per guest.
