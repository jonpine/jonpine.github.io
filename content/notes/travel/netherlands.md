### TIPS

- [Museum Pass](https://www.museum.nl/en/museumpass)
- [Amsterdam City Card](https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/tickets/i-amsterdam-city-card) *4 days*
- [Transport](https://9292.nl/en/)
- [Utrecht Bike Rent](https://willemstraatbike.nl/en)
- Instead of the 'real Dutch food' for meals, go for the snacks and sweets: Bitterballen/vleeskroket, stroopwafels, kaassouflé, poffertjes,...
- For meals, indeed go for the 'colonial' kitchens like Indonesian food. Even a lot of Dutch people claim Indonesian satay as a national dish 😅
- Eat some Indonesian food at Kantjil & De Tijger.
- Try an Indonesian rijsttafel or Surinamese roti at least once.
- As if made for the sightseer, trams #2 (direction: Nieuw Sloten) and #12 (direction: Amsteldijk) travel north-south, connecting Centraal station, Dam Square, many of my recommended hotels, and Leidseplein. Both trams then continue beyond Leidseplein to Museumplein (the stop for the Rijksmuseum and Van Gogh Museum). The entire ride takes about 15 minutes, with trams zipping by every few minutes.
- Tram #14 runs south-east, connecting Centraal station to Rembrandtplein, Waterlooplein, the Jewish Cultural Quarter, and Alexanderplein.
To get to the Anne Frank House and the Jordaan, take tram #13 or #17 from Centraal station (or Dam Square) to Westermarkt, a block from the museum and the Jordaan neighborhood.
